# Modern ricing collection

The operator approved the complete 96-image gallery on 2026-10-10. These additions bring the repository to 152 distinct wallpapers. Production admission separately binds the exact published commit, independently verified WebP assets and audited operator decision.

Every wallpaper stores tags in its candidate manifest and conversion receipt. All 96 additions have `modern`; AI artwork has `ai`; actual source aspect ratios of at least 2.2 have `ultrawide`. Full images preserve aspect ratios.

All 78 existing creator-generated AI artworks are labelled `ai` in metadata and review previews. No images were generated by RiceWM for this collection. willYEE documents Real-ESRGAN upscaling and manual selection. LibrePixels lists MidJourney, OpenAI, Leonardo AI, Pixelcut and Nero AI but does not supply exact per-image prompts or model records. Creator commitments support a rights dedication; they do not independently audit AI training data. This distinction is retained in each rights basis and evidence record.

The collection deliberately excludes recognizable people, franchise characters, third-party user contributions and logo art. Tiny fantasy figure silhouettes in some willYEE scenes are generic. Selected art has been visually reviewed for the requested desktop aesthetic and recognizable content; visual inspection cannot prove the absence of all third-party claims.

## Final verification and transfer cost

- 96 distinct names, IDs and full-image SHA256 digests; no repeated image bytes.
- All 96 candidate records pass the current wallpaper contract validator.
- All 288 derivatives match their recorded byte length and SHA256 digest, decode successfully as WebP, retain the recorded dimensions, and contain no EXIF, ICC profile or XMP.
- Every candidate, conversion receipt and evidence record has synchronized sorted tags, including `modern`.
- 9 full images preserve actual ultrawide ratios of at least 2.2.
- Full-image transfer total: **21,862,762 bytes** (~20.85 MiB).
- All 96 ordinary thumbnails: **549,418 bytes** (~536.5 KiB; ~5.6 KiB each).
- All 96 retina thumbnails: **1,679,948 bytes** (~1.60 MiB).
