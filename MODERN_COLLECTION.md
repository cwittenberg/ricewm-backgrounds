# Detailed RiceWM wallpaper collection

The current repository contains 213 candidates. Browse [README.md](README.md) for current previews and individual source/rights evidence, and [catalog/candidates.json](catalog/candidates.json) for machine-readable metadata. Repository membership does not establish live app approval.

The collection focuses on cyberpunk streets, cityscapes, science fiction, space and painted landscapes. Children’s rooms, school scenes and young-looking characters have been removed. AI-created artwork is tagged `ai`.

[Submit a wallpaper](https://account.env.now/wallpapers/submit) through a RiceWM account, with saved drafts and review feedback.
