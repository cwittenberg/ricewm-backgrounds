# Original Catppuccin Mocha designs

16 distinct original vector compositions for RiceWM.org: city skylines, orbits, moonlit landscapes, a railway platform, abstract contours, botanical scenes and glass shapes.

All sources are original SVG compositions without third-party images, fonts, logos or franchise characters. They were rendered at3840×2160, then converted into metadata-free WebP with the confined v2 processor. No source enlargement occurs. Every item stores the original vector source, source hash, converter image, three derivative hashes and descriptive tags.

Palette values match [Catppuccin Mocha](https://github.com/catppuccin/catppuccin). These are independent original designs, not official Catppuccin artwork. The publisher dedicates only its own composition rights under CC0 1.0 Universal. No AI image generator was used; the generator is code-assisted original vector art.

Public assets and candidate metadata do not imply operator approval. The approved app catalog is controlled separately by the review service.
