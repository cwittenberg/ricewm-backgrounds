# RiceWM.org wallpaper copyright process

RiceWM.org accepts only documented public-domain wallpapers or authorized CC0
dedications. Attribution is preserved for provenance and respect for creators.

## Private reports

Use [the private copyright form](https://account.env.now/wallpaper-copyright).
Include the wallpaper ID and location, identification of the original work,
supporting evidence, your name and contact information, a physical or electronic
signature, a good-faith statement that the use is unauthorized, and a statement
of accuracy and authority under penalty of perjury. The operator may need to
share a notice with the contributor or other involved parties as required by
the applicable process; it is not published in the collection.

The operator assesses reports promptly and removes disputed content when
warranted. Removal withdraws the item from the app catalog and disables new
downloads through the app. RiceWM.org records the action and notifies the
contributor where possible. A report alone does not automatically establish
infringement or cause automatic removal.

## Counter notifications

For a removed item, the private form also accepts a counter notification. Include
the original notice reference, material and former location, contact details and
signature, a statement under penalty of perjury of good-faith belief in mistake
or misidentification, consent to the required US federal jurisdiction, and consent
to accept service from the original notifier or their agent.

Where US DMCA section 512 applies, the operator must forward a valid counter
notice and follow the statutory 10–14 business-day procedure unless notified of
court action. Record forwarding, business-day calculations, court action and the
outcome privately. Counter notices never restore app approval automatically.
Formal restoration requires an operator decision after the applicable procedure.

## Repeat infringement and preservation

Substantiated repeat infringement may lead to contributor exclusion and withdrawal
of their collection entries. The operator records the evidence and decision.
Rights claims and review history are retained privately for case handling. Access
to claimant contact data is restricted to authenticated operators; personal data
is never placed in the public repository or catalog.

GitHub hosts this repository independently. Removing an app entry cannot recall
existing user downloads or erase immutable Git history. For a valid claim the
operator must also remove the current repository files and pursue history/cache
removal with GitHub where needed. GitHub's own copyright process applies to its
hosting. Do not promise that a catalog withdrawal removes every existing copy.

This procedure does not assert registered DMCA-agent status or guarantee a
statutory safe harbor. Formal designation and registration are separate operator
responsibilities. See [US Copyright Office section 512](https://copyright.gov/512/)
and [agent registration](https://copyright.gov/dmca-directory/).
